Diagnostic Facilities

Advanced Neuro-Diagnostics & Lab Tests

Precision testing and diagnostic procedures to help assess brain, nerve, cardiac, and metabolic health.

NEURO TEST

EEG (Electroencephalogram)

A non-invasive test that records electrical activity in the brain and may help evaluate seizure disorders and abnormal brain-wave activity.

Book Test →
NEURO TEST

NCS (Nerve Conduction Study)

Measures the speed and strength of electrical signals travelling through peripheral nerves to help evaluate certain nerve disorders.

Book Test →
CARDIAC TEST

ECG (Electrocardiogram)

Records the heart's electrical activity and can help identify rhythm abnormalities and other cardiac findings.

Book Test →

Specialized Blood Tests

Laboratory investigations that may help evaluate blood counts, biochemical parameters, infections, and selected metabolic conditions.
